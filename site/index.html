Enter the Hall

Kindness calls. Chaos clears.

Wander the village. Linger over a friend to hear their whisper, then step through.

We're here for the dreamers, the misfits, and the rebels who want to make the world a better place, not by following the rules, but by rewriting them.

Whether you're an artist, a wanderer, or a warrior for change, your spark of creativity matters. Join us in the chaos, and let's make a difference together, one story and one revolution at a time.

The Guild's creed

Paths through the village

  • The Star SpreadMira's tent of mirrorsCross the candlelight and cut the cards. Mira reads what the mirrors murmur and what the stars are willing to spill.
  • The Guild CalendarThe moon above the villageStay attuned to the phases of the moon, the shifting sands of season, and the fables of the fate of foxes.
  • The Notice BoardWhere the mischief gathersQuills scratch and whispers wander. Pin your plans, pass your notes, and find the friends your fortune favors.
  • The Guild HallThe door beneath the great capKnock thrice and name yourself. The Hall keeps a hearth, a ledger, and a place for every kind of rebel.
  • Reynard's StumpA fox, a lute, a likely storyLend an ear to the lute. The fox has fables to tell, and a few of them are even true.
  • The Galleon MarketThe Badger's stallHonest goods for honest galleons. Trade fair, tread kindly, and take a little of the Guild home.
  • The RaisingThe Bull at his beamsBeam by beam and day by day, the Bull builds what the village dreams. Ask him what it is all for.
  • The City on the HillWhere the Lion and the Wolf keep countDare to look toward the red towers. Learn where your heart lands between law and chaos, kindness and coin.
