Chaotic Good Vessel

Ryan A. Stewart · August 14, 2025 · 1 min read

Why are we doing this?

Look outside. It's clear that things have gotten different. When we're struggling and just looking at where we can take the next step to keep our heads above water, sometimes we look up and see that the world around us has drastically changed. That could be by design, but it could just be the natural course of a complex world.

Reynard the Fox came into life during another tumultuous and uncertain time to point a light at the way society left so many people behind. We brought him back today for the same reason. He's a medium for the message of a Chaotic Good ideology that puts people before profits, and remembers a time when rules were meant to protect us from the systems we create, not the other way around. Behind this character we're humans living our busy lives, trying to remind everybody how important they are in a society that has turned into a lifeless machine. We're here to breathe a little magic back into life, and try to make it fun to uplift others.

I'm asking that you do the same for the people in your life and community. We know that things aren't working, and the best antidote is to focus on what we have control over, which is how we treat others. This fox is just a vessel. It's the Chaotic Good ideas that unify all of us together. It's really hard being a human sometimes. So rather than focusing on our differences, let's focus on what we have in common, give one another grace and forgiveness, and try to keep smiling, even when things get tough.
