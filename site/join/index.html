Join the Guild

There's no oath and no dues. Leave your name, and now and then a letter will find you: a new tale from Reynard's Stump, a festival turning on the wheel, a card worth sitting with, and first word when the Guild Hall opens its doors and the deck goes to print.

No more than a couple of letters a month. Every one has a way out at the bottom, and leaving is as easy as joining.
