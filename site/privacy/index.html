The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-15

The short version

We collect as little as we can, we never sell or share it, and you can have it removed anytime.

If you join the Guild

We keep your email address, and, only if you give them, the name you'd like us to call you and your alignment. We also note which page you joined from and that you said you're 13 or older. We use this only to send you letters from the Guild. Signups are stored by our web host, Netlify, and copied into the service we use to send letters. Every letter has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.

The alignment test and the tarot

Your answers are scored in your own browser and never sent to us. Your browser remembers your final alignment so the Guild Hall can offer it to you later; you can clear it from the result page with "Forget my result." Tarot readings are dealt in your browser and aren't saved anywhere. A reading or result you share as a picture is made on your device and goes only where you send it.

Visits to the site

We may count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't follow you around the web. It tells us things like which pages are read and which countries visitors come from, never who you are. We don't use advertising trackers.

Videos

Videos on Reynard's Stump come from YouTube and TikTok. Nothing loads from them until you press play. Once you do, that service's own privacy policy applies to what you watch.

Children

The Guild's letters are for people 13 and older. If you're a parent and think a younger child has signed up, write to us and we'll remove them.

Removing your information

To leave, use the unsubscribe link in any letter. To have everything we hold about you deleted, or to ask what we hold, write to chaoticgoodguild@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
